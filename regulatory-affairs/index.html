Regulatory affairs

Regulatory affairs is present at every stage of a medicine's life. We shape the development plan and regulatory strategy before the first study, build the CMC, non-clinical and clinical evidence into the dossier, steer the application through authority review to approval, and then keep the authorization current through every variation, renewal and change.

Request an assessment

What we cover

End-to-end regulatory strategy for biologics, biosimilars and small molecules, from development through approval and beyond.

Development & CMC strategy

  • Regulatory design and review of comparative studies: analytical and functional biosimilarity exercises for biologics; pharmaceutical equivalence and bioequivalence programmes for small molecules
  • Regulatory oversight of CMC development, from cell-bank characterisation or drug-substance route selection, through specification setting and stability, to process validation and technology transfer
  • Comparability and change-control protocols for process, site and scale changes, for both biological and synthetic products

Dossier authoring & readiness

  • CTD technical authoring and review, Modules 1–4; eCTD preparation
  • Regulatory and quality gap analysis prior to filing
  • Pre-assessment review of dossiers from the assessor's perspective

Agency interaction & intelligence

  • Scientific advice and pre-submission meeting strategy, briefing documents and preparation (EMA, MHRA, national agencies)
  • Structured responses to agency questions and deficiency letters
  • Local requirement mapping and regional regulatory intelligence

Post-approval & lifecycle

  • Variations, amendments and renewals across regions
  • Post-approval commitments and their follow-up with agencies
  • Regulatory assessment of post-approval process, site and supplier changes

GMP & quality compliance

EU GMP · PIC/S · WHO GMP · ICH Q7–Q12

  • GMP gap assessment of manufacturing and testing sites against EU GMP (including Annex 1 and Annex 2), PIC/S and WHO requirements
  • Inspection readiness: mock inspections, pre-approval inspection preparation and front-room / back-room coaching
  • Response to inspection findings: root-cause analysis, CAPA plans and follow-up evidence packages for the agency
  • Site Master File, GMP certificate and manufacturing-authorisation applications; QP declarations for imported products
  • Pharmaceutical quality system design and review: change control, deviations, OOS, complaints and product quality review
  • Data integrity assessment
  • Supplier and CMO qualification: audits, quality and technical agreements, and ongoing oversight
  • Alignment of GMP documentation with the dossier: validation, stability and batch records that say the same thing the CTD says

Stringent regulatory authorities

European Medicines Agency

European Union · EEADossier format: eCTD

Submission routes we handle

Centralised Procedure (CP)
Single application assessed by CHMP; mandatory for biotech-derived products and biosimilars. One authorisation valid in all EU/EEA states.
Decentralised Procedure (DCP)
Simultaneous application in several member states with a Reference Member State; the usual route for generics.
Mutual Recognition Procedure (MRP)
Extension of an existing national authorisation to further member states.
National procedure
Single-country authorisation where the product is not intended for other EU markets.
Scientific advice & pre-submission
CHMP/SAWP scientific advice, pre-submission meetings and eligibility requests before filing.
CEP and ASMF (EDQM)
Certificate of Suitability or Active Substance Master File so the API can be referenced in any EU dossier.

All EU procedures, including EDQM CEP and ASMF for active substances.

Regulatory intelligence and AI in regulatory

A dossier is only as good as the guidance it was written against. We track what the agencies change, and we use AI tools, under qualified human review, to turn that information into faster, more consistent regulatory work.

Regulatory intelligence

  • Continuous monitoring of EMA, FDA, MHRA, WHO, PMDA, TGA and ICH guideline publications, concept papers and public consultations, with a focus on biologics, biosimilars and GMP
  • Impact assessment of each change on your pipeline and marketed products, with a recommended action and deadline
  • Country requirement mapping and regulatory landscape reports for market-entry decisions
  • Precedent analysis: EPARs, assessment reports, approval histories and public agency correspondence on comparable products
  • Periodic intelligence briefings tailored to your portfolio and target regions

AI in regulatory

  • AI-assisted screening of dossiers against current guidelines and agency expectations before filing: a faster first pass that our assessors then verify
  • Automated consistency checks across CTD modules: specifications, batch data, stability claims and cross-references
  • Structured first drafts of responses to agency questions, built from your source data and finalised by a regulatory professional
  • Advice on using AI in GxP-regulated work: validation expectations, data-integrity safeguards and documentation in line with emerging EMA and FDA guidance
  • Regulatory data standardisation: IDMP/SPOR-ready product data and eCTD-compliant publishing

Our principle on AI

Human accountability, always.

AI accelerates review and drafting. It never decides. Every regulatory decision and every page that reaches an agency is owned, reviewed and signed off by a qualified regulatory professional.

Finished dosage-form development

Development and manufacturing partnerships for tablets, capsules, injectables, liquids and semi-solids. Hanpars coordinates the project; partners manufacture.

Discuss a project

We run the project. Partners run the plant.

Formulation, analytical work, scale-up, stability, packaging and technology transfer rarely sit under one roof. Hanpars coordinates them across development and manufacturing partners, with the target market's regulatory requirements in view from the first batch.

Hanpars does not own a plant, does not hold a GMP certificate for one, and cannot guarantee a regulatory outcome. We offer the coordination, the partner network and the dossier.

Dosage forms we take on

Solid oral dosage forms

Tablets and capsules. Tell us the API, strength, development stage and target market.

Injectable preparations

Ampoules and vials. Include the formulation, presentation, project status and documentation needs.

Liquids and semi-solids

Oral liquids and topical preparations. Start with the formulation scope and intended use.

What we ask at the first meeting

The activities and partners depend on the answers, so we start here.

  1. The product

    API, dosage form, strength and intended presentation.

  2. The stage

    Existing formulation, dossier, technical data and development status.

  3. The market

    Where it will be registered, and what that authority will require.

  4. The support

    Formulation, scale-up, technology transfer, or a defined partner activity.

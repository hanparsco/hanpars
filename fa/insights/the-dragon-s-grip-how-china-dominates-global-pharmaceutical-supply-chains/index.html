The Dragon's Grip: How China Dominates Global Pharmaceutical Supply Chains

منتشرشده در ۲۳ اردیبهشت ۱۴۰۴، دستهٔ Supply Chain.

بازگشت به آرشیو
این مقاله با تاریخ و متن اصلی خود (به انگلیسی) نگه‌داری می‌شود. ارقام بازار، پیش‌بینی‌ها و ارجاع‌های رگولاتوری مربوط به زمان نگارش است و بازبینی نشده‌اند.

China's dominance in global pharmaceutical supply chains has reached unprecedented levels, creating both opportunities and challenges for the global healthcare ecosystem. This dominance is particularly pronounced in the Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (API) sector, where China has established itself as the world's primary source for critical pharmaceutical raw materials.

Market Share in Critical Therapeutic Categories

China's control over key API categories has reached levels that make the country an indispensable node in pharmaceutical supply chains:

Analgesics: China produces 90-95% of the Ibuprofen imported by the US and EU markets.

Antipyretics: Approximately 70% of the global supply of Paracetamol/Acetaminophen originates from Chinese manufacturers.

Antibiotics, Antimicrobials, and Antidiabetics: Critical medications like Doxycycline, Azithromycin, and Metformin have over 80% import dependency on Chinese sources in many markets.

Competitive Advantages Driving Dominance

Several interconnected factors have enabled China to achieve and maintain this dominant position:

Cost Advantages: Chinese manufacturers can price APIs 35-40% below Western competitors while maintaining FDA/EMA quality standards, creating a compelling value proposition for pharmaceutical companies under pricing pressure.

China's grip on pharmaceutical supply chains represents both a remarkable industrial achievement and a strategic challenge for global healthcare security. As the world continues to grapple with the implications of concentrated API production, China's role as the pharmacy to the world seems likely to endure, even as some diversification efforts gain momentum.

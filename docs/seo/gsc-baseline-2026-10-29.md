## Query intent
- Brand: 30 clicks. Non-brand: 28 clicks / 3,047 impressions.
- Persian non-brand: 309 queries, 25 clicks. About a sixth are consumer-intent («قیمت قرص…», «خرید…», «برای چیست», «عوارض») — patients and pharmacists, not buyers.
- B2B-intent (supplier / manufacturer / api market / cdmo / sourcing): 36 queries, 159 impressions, 0 clicks; positions mostly 10–90 (e.g. "buserelin supplier" 73, "icatibant api manufacturer" 19, "fdf cdmo services" 10.9).
- Five trailing-slash URLs (/fa/, /fa/about-us/ …) still receive 21 clicks: Google indexed them before the compatibility stubs existed; they now redirect, and should consolidate over the coming weeks.

## Reading
1. The site is being discovered fast (impressions ×100 in a week), but almost all clicks are Iranian, Persian and product-page based. The English/Chinese catalogue earns impressions, not clicks.
2. Commercial B2B queries already surface the right pages (/api/…, /fdf-cdmo) but at positions where nobody clicks. This is the cohort to improve: service pages and the API hub, not more catalogue pages.
3. Persian product pages attract patient questions. Decision needed: keep them as professional reference (current), or add a short Persian "for patients: consult your pharmacist" note so the clicks are not wasted and the pages are not mistaken for a pharmacy.

A trading company in Tehran that holds its own licences.

Hanpars is the marketing authorisation holder for the medicines it imports: the dossier, the IRC licence and pharmacovigilance are handled in-house. We source, register and coordinate; manufacturers manufacture, and the authority decides. Saying precisely who does what is how we keep projects moving.

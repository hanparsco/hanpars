Markets and sourcing

Iran is the market we supply and register in. Our sourcing reaches manufacturers in China, India, Türkiye and Europe.

Everything is organised around the Iranian market: the IFDA, the distributors and the hospitals. On the supply side we work with manufacturers in China, India, Türkiye and Europe, chosen product by product for their documentation as much as their price.

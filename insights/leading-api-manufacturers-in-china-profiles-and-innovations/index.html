Leading API Manufacturers in China: Profiles and Innovations

Published 15 October 2025 in Manufacturer Spotlight.

Back to the archive
This article is kept with its original date and commentary. Market figures, forecasts and regulatory references reflect the time of writing and were not re-verified.

China's API industry features giants producing for global markets with focus on quality and scale.

Key Players

  • Zhejiang Huahai: Leader in cardiovascular APIs, expanding to oncology with GMP-compliant facilities.
  • CSPC Pharmaceutical: Innovates in antibiotics and vitamins, investing in biotech APIs.
  • Henan Topfond: Specializes in hormone and steroid APIs with sustainable processes.
  • Shanghai Pharma: Advances in high-potency APIs for exports.

These firms drive innovation through R&D and international partnerships.

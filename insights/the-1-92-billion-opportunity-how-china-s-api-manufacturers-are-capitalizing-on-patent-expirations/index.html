The $1.92 Billion Opportunity: How China's API Manufacturers Are Capitalizing on Patent Expirations

Published 20 May 2025 in Market Analysis.

Back to the archive
This article is kept with its original date and commentary. Market figures, forecasts and regulatory references reflect the time of writing and were not re-verified.

The pharmaceutical industry is approaching a significant patent cliff, with blockbuster drugs worth over $1.92 billion facing patent expiration between 2024 and 2028. This wave of patent expirations represents a substantial opportunity for Chinese API manufacturers, who are strategically positioned to capitalize on the resulting surge in generic drug development.

Key Patent Expirations Creating Opportunities

Several high-value medications are losing patent protection in the coming years, creating specific entry points for Chinese API manufacturers:

Apixaban (Eliquis): This anticoagulant medication, which generated over $9 billion in annual sales, faces patent expiration. Chinese API manufacturers have already developed bioequivalent API production processes and are poised to supply generic manufacturers globally.

Dabigatran (Pradaxa): Another anticoagulant losing patent protection, creating opportunities in a high-value therapeutic area where Chinese manufacturers have developed specialized expertise.

The patent cliff represents a pivotal opportunity for Chinese API manufacturers to further consolidate their global market position while moving up the value chain. By strategically targeting high-value molecules coming off patent, these companies are positioning themselves not merely as suppliers of basic pharmaceutical ingredients but as sophisticated partners in the development of affordable medicines worldwide.

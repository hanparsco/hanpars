China as an Innovator in Finished Dosage Formulations: From Generics to Cutting-Edge Therapies

Published 1 September 2025 in Innovation & Technology.

Back to the archive
This article is kept with its original date and commentary. Market figures, forecasts and regulatory references reflect the time of writing and were not re-verified.

China transitions from generics leader to innovation hub, contributing ~30% to global R&D pipeline.

Rapid advancements in high-end FDF reflect policy and investment shifts.

Innovation Highlights

  • Biosimilars Boom: Numerous mAb biosimilars launched, expanding sterile capabilities.
  • Complex Generics: Progress in controlled-release and specialty products via consistency evaluations.
  • Novel Therapies: ADCs, GLP-1 analogs, and advanced injectables through collaborations.
  • Policy Support: Reforms encourage differentiation and global competitiveness.

China's innovators deliver affordable advanced FDF therapies worldwide.

How we work

A few plain principles that shape every request, from a single API enquiry to a multi-year registration programme.

Start a conversation

Clarity first, then speed.

Most delays in pharmaceutical supply come from questions nobody asked at the start: which pharmacopoeia, which dossier format, who files, who pays the fee. So we write down the need and the division of responsibilities before we talk about timing.

Integrity, quality-minded work, collaboration and technical development are the values Hanpars has always stated; here they are habits rather than slogans.

Three habits

Be precise about the need

Product, specification, quantity, market and stage, written down before we discuss the route.

Keep responsibilities visible

Sourcing and coordination are ours. Manufacturing belongs to the manufacturer; approval to the authority.

Hold one conversation

Commercial, documentation and technical questions together, so nothing is agreed that the dossier cannot support.

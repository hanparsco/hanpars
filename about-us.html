About Hanpars

A pharmaceutical trading company in Tehran that sources, imports and registers medicines and ingredients for the Iranian market.

Start a conversation

Who we are, and what we are not.

Hanpars is the trading name of Haniya Parsan Darou Co., on Zafar Street in Tehran. We source active ingredients, excipients and finished dosage forms for Iran, hold the marketing authorisations for the hospital medicines we import, and act as the in-country regulatory contact for manufacturers abroad. Suppliers are in China, India, Türkiye and Europe; customers are hospitals, distributors and companies developing their own products.

We are not a manufacturer. We source, register and coordinate; when a project needs a plant, we bring in a partner and write down who is responsible for what.

HANPARS began importing at the end of 2025 with two emergency products, misoprostol and naloxone. Through 2026 the portfolio has grown steadily as new IRC licences were granted, and the pace of imports accelerated markedly in the second half of the year.

In 2026 the company has imported more than 33 million euros’ worth of medicines — over 100 million units across 30 molecules, including contrast media, hormones, CNS medicines, anti-infectives and critical-care products — sourced from manufacturers in Türkiye, China and the United Kingdom. Further registered orders are scheduled for the coming months.

Frequently asked questions

What does Haniya Parsan Darou import?

Haniya Parsan Darou supplies and imports hospital and specialty finished medicines, active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), pharmaceutical excipients, and raw materials for pharmaceutical and food supplements for the Iranian market.

What is urgent (emergency) medicine import, and what is Hanpars' role?

Urgent import is the supply of medicines that are imported with the approval of the Iran Food and Drug Administration (IFDA) to relieve a shortage or meet an immediate treatment need. Haniya Parsan Darou holds an IFDA import licence and handles the sourcing, registration and nationwide distribution of these medicines through established pharmaceutical distributors.

Does Haniya Parsan Darou register medicines with the IFDA?

Yes. The company's regulatory team prepares the dossier, obtains the IRC code, and handles pharmacovigilance, variations and licence renewals, both for its own imported products and for foreign manufacturers.

Where are the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) sourced from?

Active ingredients and excipients are sourced from manufacturers with recognised GMP certification in China, India, Türkiye and Europe, together with the required documentation such as the CoA, DMF or CEP.

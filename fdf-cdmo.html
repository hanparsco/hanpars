Finished dosage form CDMO

Contract development and manufacturing of finished dosage forms for the Iranian market: one project team from formulation and technology transfer to IFDA registration and supply.

Discuss a project

Contract development and manufacturing

One accountable coordinator from formulation to an approved product in Iran.

A finished dosage form reaches patients only when formulation, analytical methods, scale-up, stability, packaging, regulatory approval and supply all line up. Hanpars puts one project team in charge of that chain and works with qualified development and manufacturing partners in China, Türkiye, India, Europe and Iran.

We plan every project around the market it is meant for, so the batches, the stability programme and the dossier are built for IFDA review from the start rather than adapted afterwards.

Three ways we work

Most projects start from one of three positions. The work behind both is the same: a clear brief, the right partner and a dossier built for Iran.

Foreign manufacturers

Bring an existing product to Iran

For manufacturers with an approved product who want registration and sales in Iran, through import or, where policy favours it, local manufacturing by an Iranian partner.

  • Market and regulatory route assessment
  • Letter of authorisation, IFDA dossier and IRC
  • Technology transfer to an Iranian GMP plant when local production is the better route
Iranian sponsors

Develop or manufacture a product abroad

For Iranian companies that need a formulation developed, a product manufactured under contract, or a second source qualified outside Iran.

  • Selection and qualification of a CDMO partner
  • Development, validation and stability batches
  • Supply, documentation and import into Iran
Multinational and large foreign companies

Licensed manufacturing in Iran

For originator and large generic companies whose products are better placed being manufactured under licence on the lines of an Iranian GMP plant: the licensor keeps the brand and the know-how, the Iranian plant manufactures, and Hanpars structures and runs the arrangement.

  • Selection of an Iranian plant with the right line, capacity and inspection history
  • Licence, quality and supply agreements; technology transfer and validation
  • IFDA registration as a locally manufactured product under the licensor's name

What the project covers

  1. 01

    Feasibility and regulatory route

    Reference product, patent and data position, IFDA requirements, pricing context and the most realistic path to approval.

  2. 02

    Partner selection and qualification

    Shortlist of GMP sites with the right technology and capacity, document review and audit coordination before any commitment.

  3. 03

    Formulation and analytical development

    Pre-formulation, formulation, method development and validation, comparative dissolution and bioequivalence planning.

  4. 04

    Technology transfer and scale-up

    Transfer protocols, exhibit and process-validation batches, with change control and records kept to one standard.

  5. 05

    Stability and dossier

    Stability studies for the intended climate and shelf life, and a CTD dossier assembled for IFDA review.

  6. 06

    Registration, launch and lifecycle

    IFDA submission and IRC, launch supply, variations, renewals and pharmacovigilance after approval.

How a project runs

Every project passes through the same five gates. Each one ends with a written decision before money is committed to the next.

  1. 01

    Brief and confidentiality

    Product, strength, target market, volumes and timeline; NDA signed before technical data is shared.

  2. 02

    Feasibility and route

    Regulatory route, partner shortlist, indicative timeline and the main risks, in writing.

  3. 03

    Partner and plan

    Partner qualified, responsibilities and quality agreement set out, transfer and development plan agreed.

  4. 04

    Batches and dossier

    Development, exhibit and validation batches; stability started; dossier compiled section by section.

  5. 05

    Approval and supply

    IFDA submission, questions answered, IRC issued, launch supply and lifecycle management.

Multinational and large foreign companies

Licensed manufacturing on Iranian lines

Iran manufactures most of the medicines it uses, and policy consistently favours local production. For many foreign products the realistic long-term route is therefore not import but manufacture under licence by an Iranian GMP plant, with the foreign company remaining the owner of the brand, the formulation and the quality standard.

01

Policy and pricing

Locally manufactured products are treated more favourably in registration priority, pricing and insurance coverage than the same product imported.

02

Supply continuity

Production inside the country shortens lead times and removes the product from import-licence and foreign-currency cycles.

03

Control for the licensor

The licensor approves the site, the transferred process and every change; batch release follows its specifications and audit rights are written into the agreement.

04

A base of qualified plants

Iran has a large base of GMP-licensed manufacturers covering oral solids, injectables, liquids and semi-solids, several with experience of manufacturing for international licensors.

Hanpars structures the arrangement

  • Shortlist and qualification of Iranian plants against the licensor's audit standard
  • Term sheet, licence, quality and supply agreements between the licensor and the plant
  • Regulatory route with IFDA: local manufacture under licence, registration and IRC
  • Project management of technology transfer, validation and launch; lifecycle afterwards

The licensor and the plant deliver

  • Licensor: formulation, specifications, methods, reference standards and training
  • Plant: transfer batches, process validation, stability, manufacturing and batch release
  • Both: change control and the quality agreement throughout the product life
  • API and key materials may come from the licensor or from sources it approves

Hanpars is the coordinator and, where agreed, the licensee or commercial partner; manufacturing responsibility stays with the licensed Iranian plant under its own manufacturing licence.

Who does what

Hanpars

  • Project management and a single point of contact
  • Partner search, qualification and audit coordination
  • Regulatory strategy, CTD dossier and IFDA submission
  • Import, launch supply and lifecycle management

Manufacturing partners

  • Development, manufacturing and quality control at their GMP sites
  • Batch release under their own manufacturing licence
  • Process validation and stability testing
  • Site inspections and GMP certificates

Hanpars does not own a manufacturing plant, does not hold a GMP certificate for one and cannot guarantee a regulatory outcome. Responsibilities are written down in the quality and supply agreements before work starts.
